Curwick Village Reservoir Comes Online

Major renewable energy infrastrucutre project finally comes online after years of construction.

Published: 22 Sep 2026 • Minister of Energy

Major renewable energy infrastructure in the village of Curwick has finally come online, now producing energy for the national energy grid. It's completion will ensure lower energy prices, and less reliance on non-renewable energy.

Curwick Reservoir comes online

The Curwick Village Reservoir in the Northern Territories has officially come online following years of planning, development, construction, and testing.

The new reservoir is expected to provide a reliable source of clean, green energy for communities across the region, while supporting the long-term energy needs of the Prunty District.

The project has already brought new jobs and investment to the Northern Territories, with local businesses and workers benefiting from the construction and development of the infrastructure.

The completion of the reservoir forms part of the wider Northern Prosperity Plan, announced by the Liberty Government earlier this year. The plan aims to encourage regional development, strengthen infrastructure, and support economic growth across the Northern Territories.

Government House has reaffirmed its commitment to expanding clean, green energy while ensuring that communities continue to benefit from new investment and development. The Curwick Village Reservoir is expected to play an important role in supporting the region's energy supply for years to come.
