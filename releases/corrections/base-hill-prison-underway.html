Construction begins on Base Hill Correctional Facility

Construction has begun on the Base Hill Correctional Facility in the Southern Regions following 18 months of planning.

Published: 26 October 2026 • Minister of Corrections

Construction has commenced on the new Base Hill Correctional Facility in the Southern Regions, following 18 months of planning and preparation.

Construction begins at Base Hill

Work is now underway on the Base Hill Correctional Facility, marking the beginning of the construction phase of a new corrections project in Square Country's Southern Regions.

The project has progressed through approximately 18 months of planning before construction began. The new facility will form part of the country's correctional infrastructure, with the development now moving from planning into on-site works.

Planning and development

The commencement of construction represents a significant milestone for the project. Further information about the facility's design, operational arrangements and construction programme may be released as development progresses.

Details including the facility's intended capacity, overall project cost and expected completion date have not been specified in this announcement.

Next steps

Construction will continue as the Base Hill project enters its next stage. Government House will provide further updates as additional project details become available.
